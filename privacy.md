# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 22, 2025_

Welcome to **rifftales**. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

## What Data We Collect

- **Personal Data:** We collect minimal personal information such as your username and email address for account creation and authentication purposes.
- **Equipment Data:** All equipment entries and related data you enter are stored securely in our database to provide you with persistent access to your equipment collection across devices.

## Analytics

We use **Mixpanel** to collect anonymous analytics data. This helps us understand how users interact with our site so we can improve your experience. Mixpanel may collect information such as:

- Pages visited
- Features used
- Device and browser type
- General location (city/country)

No equipment data or personally identifiable information is sent to Mixpanel.

## Data Security

- Your equipment data is stored securely in our database with industry-standard security measures.
- All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS.
- We do not share your personal equipment collection data with third parties.
- Analytics data collected by Mixpanel is anonymized and used only for improving the website.

## Cookies
We do not use cookies for storing your data. However, Mixpanel may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage data as described above.

## Your Choices

- You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
- If you wish to opt out of Mixpanel analytics, you can use browser extensions or privacy settings to block analytics scripts.
- You may also reject the cookie notice to opt out of Mixpanel.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to contact us using the contact form on [this page](https://www.rifftales.com/contact/).
